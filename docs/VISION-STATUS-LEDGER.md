## THE RETIRE LAW (how an entry dies)

1. **Entries are numbered S01, S02, … and are NEVER renumbered.** An id is a permanent
   address (the GOTCHAS.md form). New entries append; dead entries stay in place with
   their RETIRED slot filled.
2. **An entry retires ONLY by a numbered ruling, and only with evidence.** The evidence
   is the same grade the #349 census demanded: a `file:line` traced THIS round plus
   ⭐ **the world axis** — which world arms it (`?a4world=0` is the shipped world; a
   seam that exists in code but is armed by NO world has NOT retired the claim, it has
   only moved it from 「维度不存在」 to 「维度未通电」).
3. **Retiring an entry never edits its verbatim block.** The block is the historical
   record. Retirement is written into the RETIRED slot: the ruling number, the date,
   the evidence, and the world.
4. **A measurement of record cannot be retired by reading code** (#349 §0 rule 2):
   re-measuring needs a battery. Such rows stay UNVERIFIABLE until an instrument runs.
5. **Re-running the census** (`VISION-SNAPSHOT-AUDIT-2026-08.md` §HOW-TO-RE-RUN) is now
   a re-run against THIS file, entry by entry — VISION.md no longer carries the claims.
