## §0 Lineage — why assignment, and why now

1. **The measured case (v4's bequest).** The estimand arc closed end-to-end
   (#90–#123, nothing shipped) with THE FRONTIER CONCLUSION: price-shaped
   remedies SLIDE ALONG the discipline↔shape frontier, they do not MOVE it.
   Three independent evidence lines converge — (i) the zero-positive occupancy
   surfaces (statue AND containment instruments: presence has no marginal
   price where presence already exists), (ii) the offside long tail whose
   price-shaped cure AGGRAVATES (+22.8% P3a → +33.9% battery, confirmed),
   (iii) the measured discipline-for-shape trade (P3-PARTIAL bought half the
   discipline back and PAID in resolved spacing regression, −5.3%).
2. **The target, sized.** The rest-defence residual that survives every
   certified price remedy: **DEGEN-RESTDEF −16.1% [−17.2, −15.0] vs R0**
   (designated-slot occupancy, the P3p-3 battery's I5(b) instrument; halved
   from P3a's −30.2% by the remedies — the remaining half is the
   assignment-shaped core). Second family member: the offside cure inversion
   (#116.2(iii)) — out of slice-1 scope but pre-named as a later slice (§7).
3. **The forensic fact that sharpens the whole arc** (found at contract
   drafting, `src/ai/PlayerBrain.ts:1176` + `src/ai/formations.ts:169–180`):
   **R0's rest-defence discipline IS an assignment — a hand-written one.**
   `p.index === 1` is hard-coded as the rest-defence owner: formationSpot
   clamps him deep (Phase 31, gene-faced by SWEEPER `coverBias` since
   Phase 88) and the support fan explicitly excludes him
   (`!restDefence` on SupportBallCarrier). The code comment records the
   counterfactual: *"Without this the support fan pulled even the last
   outfielder to the ball, nobody covered, and every turnover was an
   uncontested breakaway."* The eye arms erode exactly this designation
   (slot occupancy 0.5876 → 0.4384), because a per-tick value gradient
   carries no concept of OWNING a job. So slice 1 is not "add a new
   mechanism" — it is: **the incumbent already proves single-owner
   assignment buys the discipline; replace the hard-coded index with an
   emergence-compatible, gene-weighted, percept-clean assignment primitive
   the eye can keep.**
4. **The charter (carried from #33/#116, verbatim constraints).** A4 doctrine
   = *shared priors that let a player act safely on less evidence*. A prior
   may buy **latency and priority, never reveal an unobserved body**. Roles
   (Stage III v3) were A4's slice 0 — division of labour as minimal doctrine.
   The layer above ROLE is JOB ASSIGNMENT: 野球开场的三十秒 ("你踢后卫,我顶
   前面") is the cheapest, most consequential act of coaching — breaking
   symmetry before play.

## §1 The claim under test (the estimand)

**H-A4.1 (the frontier claim):** a pre-match, per-team, gene-weighted
assignment of ONE job — rest-defence ownership — lets an eye-consuming team
recover the assignment-shaped discipline residual **without paying shape**:
on the primary contrast (§5), DEGEN-RESTDEF improves resolvedly while NO
shape limb (spacing, dupRun, roleMixTV, watchability DEGEN family) resolves
negative. That conjunction is precisely what every price-shaped remedy
measurably failed to achieve — it is the operational meaning of "MOVING the
frontier".

Falsifiable both ways, FAIL modes pre-named in §6. This is a MEASUREMENT
arc: nothing ships from slice 1 regardless of verdict (Road B).

## §2 Mechanism — what slice 1 builds (all dormant)

* **M1 — the assignment primitive.** A per-team, pre-match job table with
  exactly ONE job type in slice 1: `REST_DEFENCE_OWNER`. Fixed at kickoff
  (and at each restart-of-half), static for the whole match. No in-match
  reassignment, no handoff (that is A3-mutual-resolution territory, §7).
* **M2 — owner selection, gene-weighted.** The owner is a deterministic
  argmax over the team's outfield bodies of a score built ONLY from the
  world's own variables (the body's own attributes and role — the same
  variables v3's census axes use), weighted by a NEW per-team assignment
  gene family (policyGenome idiom: born at neutral defaults so generation 0
  behaviour is earned, bounded, evolvable later). Capability, not mandate:
  a team whose genes weight the assignment to zero effectively declines the
  doctrine — and pays whatever the world charges for that (the §3-realism
  "有的队不夹击,活该" genre). Slice-1 batteries pin the born-default
  weights (measurement of the mechanism's VALUE; the selection/PAYS proof
  is a later, separate step — two-step as standard).
* **M3 — execution through the eye seam.** The owner's station decision
  consumes a JOB TERM through the established eye.v4 seam pattern (P3p-0
  build idiom: dormant flag, `=== true` opt-in, consumption only at priced
  moments under the banked in-support law). The term enters in **goal-value
  units** from the A4-P1 vacancy census (§4) via the admitted P1 calibration
  prices (opponent deep entry 0.0435 [0.0308, 0.0558], box entry 0.1952
  [0.1662, 0.2235] concessions/event) — **NO FREE HAND-WEIGHTS** (v4 I-series
  inherited verbatim). The job's geometry anchors on the world's OWN
  designated-slot machinery (the same slot the I5(b) instrument reads, i.e.
  the formation base spot + the SWEEPER gene's possession face) — no fresh
  authored coordinates.
* **M4 — the release half.** Non-owners carry NO new term. The frontier
  hypothesis is exactly that one reliable owner beats N unreliable ones;
  the release is the ABSENCE of the diffuse duty, not a new mechanism. (In
  the incumbent, release exists as the hard-coded `!restDefence` support-fan
  exclusion; under assignment it generalizes: the fan excludes the OWNER,
  whoever the genes picked.)
* **M5 — percept plumbing.** The assignment table is team-side doctrine
  state: every teammate may read the AGREEMENT's content (who owns which
  job — that is what the pre-match thirty seconds establishes; VISION §1:
  教练战术 = 本队共同知道的 prior). It carries ZERO live state — where the
  owner IS right now remains observable only through each body's own
  perception. Opponents cannot read it at all; they may only infer it from
  外显 behaviour (and slice 1 builds no opponent-inference consumer).

## §3 Invariants (binding on every A4 stage; v4 I-series inherited)

* **I-A1 (percept honesty / the charter).** The assignment buys latency and
  priority only. It never reveals an unobserved body, never updates during
  play, never carries positions, intents or any live state. Own-job
  knowledge is own-state; teammates' job knowledge is agreement content
  (static, pre-match); opponents get nothing.
* **I-A2 (no hand-coded tactics).** The primitive is a capability with
  gene-gated use. WHO owns = gene-weighted argmax over the world's own
  variables. WHAT the job is worth = measured (the vacancy census), never
  authored. WHERE the job lives = the world's own designated-slot machinery,
  never fresh coordinates. No role privilege is hard-wired (the DF may
  usually WIN the argmax under sensible genes — that is emergence's
  business, not an index check).
* **I-A3 (no free hand-weights).** Every term the owner's eye consumes is in
  goal-value units through measured calibration, or it does not enter.
  Gene weights are not hand knobs (they are evolution's seat); instrument
  constants are frozen in pre-registrations before any run.
* **I-A4 (job-menu honesty — the VISION-audit amendment, §8 F1).** Job
  TYPES are substrate dimensions: hand-built, like gene dimensions and
  percept dimensions (VISION §1 诚实张力 — 涌现的是权重,不是维度本身).
  Slice 1's verdict is therefore about the assignment MECHANISM's value,
  never a claim that our job taxonomy is right or complete. Later slices
  must let job content/count vary under genes; a fixed one-job menu may not
  silently grow into a hand-authored playbook.
* **I-A5 (Road B).** Everything dormant behind explicit `=== true` opt-ins;
  the production fingerprint `57b0bdab…c673` unchanged; nothing ships from
  this arc without its own A/B under §6 discipline.
* **I-A6 (pre-registration discipline).** Gates freeze before runs;
  predicates never change after sight; FAILs reported as-is; sizing before
  floors; the attainability-knee N rule; wall-derived N_MAX; no optional
  stopping.
* **I-A7 (dormancy proof).** Flag-off byte-identity (the X-MERGE-IDENT
  genre): with every A4 flag off, the sim's decision stream is byte-identical
  to HEAD. Asserted in tests before any battery runs.

## §4 A4-P1 — THE VACANCY CENSUS (fork C, folded in as calibration)

The #123.5 fork's option C (the fork-and-abandon absence census) is not a
competitor to this contract — it is its calibration stage. Before the owner
can consume a job term, the job's price must be measured:

* **Estimand:** the concession price of rest-defence VACANCY, in goal-value
  units — natural windows during own-possession phases, conditioned on the
  designated slot being occupied vs vacant (the world's own variation; the
  I5(b) instrument REUSED verbatim, no new geometry), priced through the P1
  calibration events (deep entries, box entries). This is the measurement
  the v4 occupancy censuses could NOT deliver (they priced presence where
  presence exists — zero-positive by construction, #109.3); the vacancy
  census prices ABSENCE where absence occurs.
* **Form:** read-only fork-and-observe at held lattices (#91 idiom: held
  LATTICE, never the incumbent designation); stratified within context×role
  (the #94.3 Simpson rule); receipts per record; sizing smoke first.
* **Output:** the job-term table (goal-value units) the M3 seam consumes,
  SHA-pinned, committed with the census data.
* **Gate to proceed:** the census must yield a MONOTONE, resolvedly nonzero
  vacancy price (CI excluding zero on the pooled primary cell). A null or
  non-monotone price STOPS the arc at P1 (the frontier claim would have no
  measured term to enter through — building M1–M4 anyway would violate
  I-A3), and returns to the user with the finding.

* **Seeds:** bands **11.7M–12.3M reserved to A4 slice 1** (v4 consumed

## §6 FAIL modes, pre-named

* **F-SLIDE:** discipline improves but a shape limb resolves negative on
  `ASSIGN − R3p` — assignment as built is still price-shaped in effect; the
  frontier did not move. Diagnose against the M3 term (is the seam leaking
  a gradient rather than an ownership?) before any redesign; no third
  mechanism iteration without a user ruling (the #106.6 rule carries).
* **F-NULL:** no resolved DEGEN-RESTDEF effect — the owner's lever is too
  weak (seam consumption too rare under the in-support law, or the job term
  too small in goal-value units). Report with the decision ledger; STOP.
* **F-SHAPE-ONLY:** shape improves, discipline doesn't — the release half
  did the work, not the ownership. Honest partial; STOP and report.
* In every case: FAILs reported as-is, the arc returns to the user, nothing
  ships, and the vacancy-census asset stays banked regardless.

4. No selection/PAYS proof (whether evolution SELECTS the assignment genes
   is the standard two-step's second step, after value is proven).
5. No claim that the job taxonomy is right (I-A4); no claim about any job
   type beyond rest-defence ownership.
6. Nothing ships; no production behaviour change; the fingerprint stands.

## §8 VISION audit record (clause-by-clause, at drafting — the #91 form)

* **F1 (job-menu genre risk).** VISION §1's live violation is "阵型/位置 =
  我们画死的菜单". A hand-defined job menu is the same genre. FINDING:
  slice 1's one-job menu is admissible ONLY as a substrate dimension under
  the 诚实张力 clause (dimensions hand-built, weights emerge) — and VISION
  itself names rest defence as a real transitional shape that should
  emerge (§1 攻守两套形状: "攻转守/守转攻有过渡形状(rest defence 等)").
  AMENDED into I-A4 (job-menu honesty): mechanism-value claim only; later
  slices must open the menu to genes.
* **F2 (coach-prior anchor — direct support).** VISION §1 (2026-07-22 user
  completion): 教练/球队可以拥有本队共同知道的原则、优先级、结构约束;它们是
  球员形成私人意图的共同 prior,不是逐 tick 广播坐标的 commander;对手只能
  从外显推断。 The assignment primitive is exactly this shape. Locked into
  M5 + I-A1 (pre-match static, zero live state, opponent-opaque).
* **F3 (emergence soul).** 底座给"允许 X"的能力,不替球队"定 X"。 M2's
  gene-weighted decline path (weights→0 = no anchor, pay the price) keeps
  assignment a capability. The hard-coded `p.index === 1` incumbent is the
  thing being made emergence-compatible, not the thing being copied.
* **F4 (no dead genes).** VISION §1 底座属性完备+每项真有影响: the new
  assignment genes must have a measurable hook (the owner argmax + M3 term)
  — the battery is the sensitivity probe. Decorative gene = FAIL at P2
  review.
* **F5 (watchability hard gate).** VISION §2: any mechanism that makes the
  game 更乱抢/更难看 fails EVEN IF its own metric wins. The frontier
  predicate (§5) makes this the PASS condition itself, not a side check.
* **F6 (realism).** Rest defence / +1 cover on turnovers is a real-football
  mechanism (VISION §3.0's spirit; the Phase-31 comment documents the
  breakaway pathology it prevents). The 街机取舍 list is untouched.
* **F7 (work discipline).** VISION §6: probe-first, A/B before ship, honest
  reverts. Slice 1 is measurement-only by construction; any future shipping
  goes through §6 discipline separately.
* **Verdict: PASS with one amendment (F1 → I-A4).** No clause of this
  contract requires weakening any VISION anchor.
