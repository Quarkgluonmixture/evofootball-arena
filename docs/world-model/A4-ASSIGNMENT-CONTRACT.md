## §0 Lineage — why assignment, and why now

1. **The measured case (v4's bequest).** The estimand arc closed end-to-end
   (#90–#123, nothing shipped) with THE FRONTIER CONCLUSION: price-shaped
   remedies SLIDE ALONG the discipline↔shape frontier, they do not MOVE it.
   Three independent evidence lines converge — (i) the zero-positive occupancy
   surfaces (statue AND containment instruments: presence has no marginal
   price where presence already exists), (ii) the offside long tail whose
   price-shaped cure AGGRAVATES (+22.8% P3a → +33.9% battery, confirmed),
   (iii) the measured discipline-for-shape trade (P3-PARTIAL bought half the
   discipline back and PAID in resolved spacing regression, −5.3%).
2. **The target, sized.** The rest-defence residual that survives every
   certified price remedy: **DEGEN-RESTDEF −16.1% [−17.2, −15.0] vs R0**
   (designated-slot occupancy, the P3p-3 battery's I5(b) instrument; halved
   from P3a's −30.2% by the remedies — the remaining half is the
   assignment-shaped core). Second family member: the offside cure inversion
   (#116.2(iii)) — out of slice-1 scope but pre-named as a later slice (§7).
3. **The forensic fact that sharpens the whole arc** (found at contract
   drafting, `src/ai/PlayerBrain.ts:1176` + `src/ai/formations.ts:169–180`):
   **R0's rest-defence discipline IS an assignment — a hand-written one.**
   `p.index === 1` is hard-coded as the rest-defence owner: formationSpot
   clamps him deep (Phase 31, gene-faced by SWEEPER `coverBias` since
   Phase 88) and the support fan explicitly excludes him
   (`!restDefence` on SupportBallCarrier). The code comment records the
   counterfactual: *"Without this the support fan pulled even the last
   outfielder to the ball, nobody covered, and every turnover was an
   uncontested breakaway."* The eye arms erode exactly this designation
   (slot occupancy 0.5876 → 0.4384), because a per-tick value gradient
   carries no concept of OWNING a job. So slice 1 is not "add a new
   mechanism" — it is: **the incumbent already proves single-owner
   assignment buys the discipline; replace the hard-coded index with an
   emergence-compatible, gene-weighted, percept-clean assignment primitive
   the eye can keep.**
4. **The charter (carried from #33/#116, verbatim constraints).** A4 doctrine
   = *shared priors that let a player act safely on less evidence*. A prior
   may buy **latency and priority, never reveal an unobserved body**. Roles
   (Stage III v3) were A4's slice 0 — division of labour as minimal doctrine.
   The layer above ROLE is JOB ASSIGNMENT: 野球开场的三十秒 ("你踢后卫,我顶
   前面") is the cheapest, most consequential act of coaching — breaking
   symmetry before play.

## §2 Mechanism — what slice 1 builds (all dormant)

> **#137 RESCOPE (2026-08-02, user-ruled A′ after the P1/P1b STOPs and the
> #136 semantics correction).** The original M1–M4 (a one-job table,
> `REST_DEFENCE_OWNER`, owner-argmax, a priced job term) are SUPERSEDED —
> P1b measured the job-LAW form redundant-to-costly wherever a positional
> prior already covers, and the user's 摇摆人 argument showed a job enum
> cannot represent real positions. The primitive is now the PRIOR itself:

* **M1′ — the home-region prior primitive.** Per-team, pre-match, per-head:
  each body carries a **coarse 2D home region** — center + independent
  depth/width extents, in the team-local ball-relative frame — as the
  content of the kickoff agreement ("你踢后卫" = your home is the back;
  the 摇摆人 = an elongated mid-front region). Fixed at kickoff, static for
  the match (in-match changes are the coach-loop rung, §7). Both axes are
  IN the representation (deconfliction — the user's own definition of 位置
  — needs lateral separation; 强弱侧 lives on width); which axis a body
  obeys harder is selection's business, never wired.
* **M2′ — content and genes.** The home distribution (everyone's centers,
  extents) plus per-body obedience strength and personal offsets are the
  evolvable content: a NEW gene family (policyGenome idiom — born at the
  INCUMBENT-EQUIVALENT distribution so generation 0 behaviour is unchanged
  and every divergence is earned; bounded; evolvable). Capability, not
  mandate: near-zero obedience = a team that in effect plays without
  agreements and pays whatever the world charges (the "有的队不夹击,活该"
  genre; an all-forwards home distribution is a legal gamble). Slice-1
  batteries pin born-default content — the VALUE of the mechanism is the
  measurand; the selection/PAYS proof is the standard second step.
* **M3′ — execution: soft blending, never clamps.** The prior enters each
  body's eye as a **soft bias term** on station value (distance-decayed
  toward the home region, in the eye's native score units), consumed
  through the established eye.v4 seam pattern (dormant flag, `=== true`
  opt-in, priced moments under the banked in-support law). **Clamps are
  the certified disease** (P1b: the incumbent's `Math.min` pin priced
  ≤ 0): a strong local gradient must be able to legally win. Strength: a
  GENE in the shipped form (genes are evolution's seat, not hand knobs —
  I-A3); a pre-registered DOSE GRID in instruments (P1c sweeps it — the
  dose-response ladder IS the monotone gate axis).
* **M4′ — the expectation half.** Deconfliction is carried by the SHARED
  content: every teammate reads the agreement (M5), so the eye's crowding/
  duplication terms can price "that lane is his" without any exclusion
  law. No body carries a special-case rule; the incumbent's hard-coded
  `!restDefence` fan exclusion has NO successor (P1b measured it
  redundant) — cover and separation both emerge from priors + flux.
* **AMENDMENT #130 (2026-08-02, the user's realism challenge at the A4-P1
  return — "凭啥他被钉在后面?现实里面不是有高位逼抢的球队中后卫都到中圈了").**
  The job's content splits into three authorities and slice 1 must not
  conflate them: **WHO** owns the job (M2, gene argmax — the slice-1
  measurand), **WHETHER** the assignment exists (M1, the primitive), and
  **HEIGHT** — how high the rest line sits. HEIGHT is a STRATEGIC VARIABLE
  in real football (the high-press identity parks the last man at the
  centre circle, coupled to the offside trap and counter-press; the deep
  libero is a different identity), but the incumbent substrate FORBIDS it:
  the Phase-88 `coverBias` possession face spans only `[−8, −16]` local —
  no genome can hold a halfway-height rest line (a VISION §1 "替球队定 X"
  violation, now anchored in VISION §3.1). Slice 1 therefore builds the
  HEIGHT hook as a gene dimension with the full realistic range (halfway
  down to the current deep band), **born at the incumbent-equivalent
  default** so generation 0 is unchanged and any high-line identity is
  EARNED by selection (the `trapBias` gene, Phase 109, is its pre-existing
  partner). The P3 battery pins born-default height (it isolates OWNERSHIP
  value); the VALUE of height variation is a NAMED LATER SLICE (§7), not a
  slice-1 claim — the hook exists so nothing is foreclosed, per I-A4.
  *(#137 note: under M1′ this amendment is NATIVE — HEIGHT is simply the
  depth center/extent of the home region; the [−8,−16] cap dissolves with
  the clamp itself.)*
* **M5 — percept plumbing (unchanged in kind).** The agreement is team-side
  doctrine state: every teammate may read its CONTENT (each body's agreed
  home — that is what the pre-match thirty seconds establishes; VISION §1:
  教练战术 = 本队共同知道的 prior; per #132 the representation is per-head
  common knowledge, identical at kickoff). It carries ZERO live state —
  where a body IS right now remains observable only through each body's own
  perception. Opponents cannot read it at all; they may only infer it from
  外显 behaviour (and slice 1 builds no opponent-inference consumer).

## §3 Invariants (binding on every A4 stage; v4 I-series inherited)

  priority only. It never reveals an unobserved body, never updates during

  knowledge is own-state; teammates' job knowledge is agreement content
  (static, pre-match); opponents get nothing.

  the BLENDING RULE only (the dimension layer, VISION 诚实张力). Never

  root-replacement path for VISION §1's head violation). WHAT the prior is
  worth = measured (P1c), never authored. Generation 0 = the
  incumbent-equivalent distribution; divergence earned. No role privilege
  hard-wired.
* **I-A3 (no free hand-weights).** Every term the owner's eye consumes is in
  goal-value units through measured calibration, or it does not enter.
  Gene weights are not hand knobs (they are evolution's seat); instrument
  constants are frozen in pre-registrations before any run.
* **I-A4 (representation honesty — §8 F1, re-formed by #136/#137).** The
  REGION PARAMETERIZATION (convex region, center + two extents, soft decay)

  verdict is about the prior MECHANISM's value, never a claim that this
  parameterization is right or final (a convex region cannot express a
  bimodal home — an honest bound, §7). Later slices may extend the
  representation; it may never silently grow into a hand-authored playbook.
* **I-A5 (Road B).** Everything dormant behind explicit `=== true` opt-ins;
  the production fingerprint `57b0bdab…c673` unchanged; nothing ships from

* **I-A6 (pre-registration discipline).** Gates freeze before runs;
  predicates never change after sight; FAILs reported as-is; sizing before
  floors; the attainability-knee N rule; wall-derived N_MAX; no optional

* **I-A7 (dormancy proof).** Flag-off byte-identity (the X-MERGE-IDENT

## §6 FAIL modes, pre-named

* **F-SLIDE:** discipline improves but a shape limb resolves negative on
  `PRIOR − R3p` — the prior as built is still price-shaped in effect; the
  frontier did not move. Diagnose against the M3′ blend (is the bias term
  acting as a gradient rather than an anchor?) before any redesign; no
  further mechanism iteration without a user ruling (the #106.6 rule
  carries).
* **F-NULL:** no resolved DEGEN-RESTDEF effect — the prior's lever is too
  weak (consumption too rare under the in-support law, or the granted
  strength range too low). Report with the decision ledger; STOP.
* **F-SHAPE-ONLY:** shape improves, discipline doesn't — deconfliction
  (M4′) did the work, not the anchor. Honest partial; STOP and report.
* In every case: FAILs reported as-is, the arc returns to the user, nothing
  ships, and the vacancy-census asset stays banked regardless.
