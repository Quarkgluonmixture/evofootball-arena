### in plain football language

BF-T0 put a shut door in the engine. Behind it there is a price: **when a body's hips are more
than about 90° away from where it is running, it runs at 70 % of the speed it asked for** — and
a nearly-straight run pays essentially nothing, because the shape is flat near zero.

This exam opens that door on 506 matches and asks the two questions any body law owes before
anyone plays it.

1. **Does the price actually BITE on a real pitch — and where the census said it would?**
   BF-C0 counted the ground this world runs off-heading. If the law is real, the ticks spent
   going, in part, **backwards** should get **slower**; the ticks spent running **nearly straight
   ahead** should **not**. Both are measured, and both are said honestly to be *selection*
   statistics: charging for a direction changes who ends up in each bin.
2. **Is it still football?** Goals inside a band, passing not worse, turnovers not up. A price
   that makes the game unwatchable is not a price worth paying, whatever the physics says.

Beside those two, without gating anything: **how often the price was actually charged** (the
live coverage — the share of moving ticks on which the factor was below 1, recomputed from the
same public fields the seam read); **who pays** (the misalignment shares by role — BF-C0 said
this is first and foremost a law about goalkeepers, so: does the keeper stop shuffling side-on?
does any outfield class start backpedalling?); **what the keeper does** (goals, saves, the
ground he covers); **what the defence does** (乱跑 — the marker's assigned man changing — and
whether marking coverage survives); **the user's own three complaints** (passing into an
opponent, receiving side-on, 挤人); and **E4** (forward share, third-man, overlaps, chain).

And two things the empty-book pair cannot answer are measured beside it, at the same precision:
the **DOSED** pair (the world the user actually plays, with the recognition books matured) and
**two other depths** (k = 0.60, a harder price; k = 0.80, a softer one) — the band an `agility`
attribute would have supplied, stood in for.
