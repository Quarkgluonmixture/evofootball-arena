## §0 THE QUESTION, in football

⭐ **一个刚拿到球的人,身后到底有没有人可以回?** The contract's structural sentence is a claim about
the world: *"even an unpressed carrier has NOWHERE BACKWARD TO GO — the world cannot circulate
because the circulation options do not exist."* That sentence has never been measured. This stage
measures it — at the moment it matters (the touch), in the world the user actually plays
(`?a4world=7`), with the engine's own definition of "could that ball get to him".

Four photographs, one battery:

1. **BEHIND-BALL OPTIONS** — at every reception and every pressed-carrier moment, how many
   team-mates are behind the ball AND actually passable to.
2. **THE DIRECTION MIX** — of the passes that come off, how many go forward / sideways / back.
3. **THE TERMINAL CENSUS** — how possessions actually END.
4. **E7 AT WORLD GRAIN** — where the bodies are, relative to the ball, when someone receives it.

### ⭐⭐ Instrument 1 — THE BEHIND-BALL OPTION, as a LADDER of engine verdicts

No rung is invented by this probe. Every one is the engine's own machinery answering its own
question (#256.2 commensurability):

| rung | what it asks | whose code answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 POSITION** | is the team-mate BEHIND the ball line (toward our own goal)? `Δ = localX(mate) − localX(ball)`; **BEHIND** ⇔ `Δ ≤ −2 m`, **LATERAL** ⇔ `|Δ| < 2 m`, **AHEAD** ⇔ `Δ ≥ +2 m` | ⭐ the **±2 m band is Q07's own**, EXTRACTED at run time from the engine's forward-pass line in `src/sim/mechanics.ts` — never typed in the probe |
| **L2 THE BALL GETS THERE** | does the engine's own ground-pass prediction say the ball ARRIVES at all (its friction, its launch-speed law)? | `predictGroundPass` via `evaluatePassAffordance` (`flight.reachable`) |
| **L3 THE RECEIVER WINS THE RACE** | does the intended receiver reach the landing point before ANY opponent? `arrivalMargin > 0` | `evaluatePassAffordance` — a SIGN test on the engine's own quantity, no invented threshold |
| **L4 THE CORRIDOR IS NOT CUT** | can any opponent meet the ball ON ITS PATH? | `evaluatePassCorridorInterception` — `earliestFeasiblePoint !== null` ⇔ some defender's ETA beats the ball at some sampled corridor point (again a sign test, inside the engine's own sampler) |
| **L5 (reported beside)** | is the option inside the engine's OWN pass-choice window (6–30 m, keepers excluded)? | `passChoiceCandidateGids` — the window the live perceived chooser enumerates over |

⭐ **THE PUBLISHED "OPTION" IS L1 ∧ L2 ∧ L3 ∧ L4.** Every weaker rung is published beside it, so
the **drop-off itself is a measured face**: how many bodies are behind the ball, how many of those
the ball can even reach, how many win the race, how many survive the corridor. A census that
published only the loosest rung would have answered a different question, and the ladder is what
makes "which missing option binds first" arguable from arithmetic instead of taste.

The snapshot fed to both evaluators is `oraclePerceptionSnapshot` — the FULL-TRUTH snapshot the
engine itself ships *for offline oracle probes* ("never a live perception path"), built from
`capturePerceptionTruth(match)` with the match's own `reachProfiles()`. ⚠ **This is a CAPABILITY
census, not a perception census**: it asks *could the ball get there*, never *did the carrier see
him*. The perception half belongs to the INFO-DOCTRINE contract and is out of scope here (§4).

### The three populations

| population | definition |
| --- | --- |
| **reception** | every tick at which the ball's owner CHANGES while `phase === 'playing'` — #173's ownership-episode start. The census is taken **for the receiver**. |
| **pressed reception** | a reception whose receiver has an opponent inside the pressure radius (**4.2 m** — `TOUCH_CONTROL_DIST`, the #173/Q14 switch, imported not typed). |
| **pressed-carrier moment** | a NON-reception carrier tick, sampled every **12 ticks (0.2 sim-s)**, at which the carrier is pressed. ⚠ A declared SAMPLE, not every tick. |

### Instrument 2 — the direction mix, and exactly what is reused

**FORWARD is R-乙 Q07 VERBATIM**: the engine's own `team.stats.passesForward` counter, attributed
to an individual attempt by its **per-tick delta** at the tick the attempt is registered. Q07's own
semantics note (quoted in §CORRECTIONS-READ) records that the complement POOLS backward and
lateral. This stage splits that complement with **the same ±2 m rule on the same displacement**
(`localX(target) − localX(passer)`), measured on the positions of the tick **before** the strike,
and:

* the **FORWARD** share always uses the **ENGINE'S** verdict, never the probe's re-derivation;
* the probe's re-derivation is used **only inside the engine's pooled complement**, so a
  disagreement can only mis-file a pass **within** backward-vs-lateral, never into or out of
  forward;
* the **agreement rate** between the two is published (`q07Receipt.agreementShare`), so the size of
  that exposure is a measured number rather than an assurance.

A pass is **COMPLETED** when the engine's own `passesCompleted` fires and `lastCompletedPass` names
this attempt's passer. Attribution book-keeping is a gate (`gQ07`): every engine pass is either
attributed to an observed attempt or counted as UNATTRIBUTED, and neither the forward nor the
completion count may exceed the engine's own.

### Instrument 3 — the spell terminal census

The segmentation is **#173 / R-乙 Q01 VERBATIM** (ported from `scripts/probes/l3-t2-armed-world-read.ts`,
itself the #173 walk): a maximal same-team ownership interval while `phase === 'playing'`,
SUSPENDED (not ended) while the ball is loose in play, ended by an opponent establishing ownership /
the phase leaving `playing` / full time; `openPlay` origin is the headline population; #173's
6-tick foul lookahead is inherited unchanged.

⭐ **THE TERMINAL CLASS IS READ FROM THE ENGINE'S OWN EVENT TAPE, SPELL-SCOPED.** A tackle sets
`ball.owner = null` and the ball SQUIRTS — the spell does not end until an opponent establishes
ownership, which can be many ticks later. A fixed window around the terminating tick therefore
misses tackles entirely (**it did: the first build measured 0 tackles**, which is how the tape
design was found). The class is the **LATEST qualifying engine event inside the spell's own span** —
no invented window:

| terminator | class, from the tape |
| --- | --- |
| opponent establishes ownership | `tackled` / `intercepted` / `badTouch` (opp `tackles`, opp `interceptions`, own `miscontrols`) → else `lostOther` |
| ball leaves play | `shot` / `forcedLong` (own `shots`; own `clearances` or `longBalls`) → else `outOfPlay` |
| foul (#173 lookahead) | `foulWon` / `foulCommitted` |
| goal / full time | `goal` / `matchEnd` |

⚠ **THE CLASS IS THE LAST RECORDED EVENT**, so a bad touch that an opponent then intercepts files
as `intercepted`. That is a stated convention, not a claim about which caused which.

### Instrument 4 — E7 at world grain

The distribution of team-mate longitudinal offsets from the ball at reception moments: the share
behind / lateral / ahead, and the mean Δ. The E7 row ("support is ALWAYS ahead of the ball";
aheadBias 0.75/0.35) is a claim about a mechanism's SETTINGS; this measures where the bodies
actually end up.

## §NRULE — the sizing, frozen before the battery

**N = 300 seeds per arm, paired.** The binding precision term is the per-reception option count,
whose cluster is the MATCH (~150+ receptions per match ⇒ ~45,000 receptions per arm); 300 clusters
is the size at which the cluster-bootstrap CI on a per-reception mean is comfortably inside the
0–1 vs 2–3 distance the #246 pre-registration turns on. ⚠ No formal MDE is claimed: **nothing here
is scored**, so N buys descriptive precision, not power against a hypothesis. The wall cost was measured on
the smoke (≈0.2 s per walk ⇒ ≈4 minutes for 1,250 walks including the G-DET second run and the
non-perturbation controls) and never bound the choice.

Estimator: **cluster bootstrap over match seeds** (#20), **2,000 resamples**, percentile 95 % CI,
**ratio of sums**. One resample-index matrix is drawn once and shared by every face and both arms,
so the levels and the contrast are the same resampled worlds.
