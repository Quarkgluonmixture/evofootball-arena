## §0 The map-vs-reality audit (the user's question, answered before drafting)

Today's pass pricer reads: lane (STATIC corridor openness at the strike
instant) · open (openness at the aim, same instant) · gain (LINEAR forward
metres) · d · hand bonuses (wall/third-man/overlap/mode) · riskTolerance and
passing attr on the segregated through-ball/loft paths. Against the real

already banked:

1. **风险侧近乎空白.** Real: "can this be cut out IN FLIGHT" — defenders'
   arrival over the ball's travel TIME, and "what does losing it HERE cost"
   — turnover danger by location. Ours: the static lane read only; NO
   flight-time exposure term (H-T1s-a: aggressive strikes outscore their

   see the single largest goal factory it feeds).

   metres toward goal, everywhere equal.
3. **收球侧上下文缺失.** Real: "what can HE do with it there" — the
   receiver's plight on arrival (pressure closing during flight, facing
   which way). Ours: nothing (the exams measured it: ~82 % of first

Verdict: 地图与真实不一样,且三处都有已入库的仪器直指. The fix is NOT to
