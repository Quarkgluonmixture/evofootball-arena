## §5 — ⭐ WHY THE N-FREEZE IS LOAD-BEARING (and why this stage does NO sizing)

`N = 8,000` is frozen by #162.3 and is **not derived here**. The reason is leg (c).

Leg (c) is a **negative** reading — *the deep account must not RESOLVE worse*. Negative
readings get EASIER as power falls: shrink N, the CI widens, the lower bound slides below
zero, and the leg passes **because the exam could not see**. A stage that sized its own N
off its own smoke could therefore buy leg (c) with a small number, and nothing in the
output would look wrong. Freezing N at the commander level, before any seed of this stage
ran, forecloses that entirely.

**8,000 is also exactly the S2-P1 census N** (#160.3/#161.1: 8,000 matches, 96,000 admitted
forks). So the confirmatory exam carries the **same power as the source estimates it is
confirming** — the anchors in §4.1 and the numbers this exam produces are read on the same
scale, and a discrepancy between them cannot be blamed on the exam being smaller.

**Consequently the bounded smoke of this stage sizes NOTHING.** It exists to answer two
mechanical questions before a multi-hour detached run: *what does a match cost in wall
now that each fork runs three branches instead of six*, and *does every counter and every
hard gate still plumb through on the new seed block*. The `confirm-smoke` mode therefore
computes **no contrast, no σ̂, no MDL, no N arithmetic and no gate leg at all** — there is
no number in it that could inform anything (#105.4, I-A6). Its per-arm LEVELS are published
only as evidence that the instruments populate.
