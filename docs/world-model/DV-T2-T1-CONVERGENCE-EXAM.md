### ⭐ The replicate, and what a "team" is here

A **replicate** = **ONE persistent `dvLearnedBooks` set** (two books, one per side) shared across
its **whole match sequence**. `League.startSeason()` **never fires** — the probe drives the
sequence directly — so **M-DV2.2's season-reset clause is HONOURED, not amended**: the reset is
the League's act, and a probe-driven sequence is **one long season in substance**, declared as such
(#257.3(a)).

⭐ **THE LEARNER IS THE SIDE, AND THE SQUADS ARE REDRAWN PER FIXTURE** — exactly as the census
draws them. This is a deliberate structural choice, declared: it keeps the book's sampling
population **identical to the yardstick's population**. A fixed-roster sequence would make each
book converge to **one matchup's** truth rather than the world's, which would arm **F-DV2-b**
spuriously (a non-census shape that is an artefact of the fixture list, not of the label).

## §M — ⭐⭐ THE EX-ANTE SIZING, EVERY STEP, FROZEN BEFORE ANY BATTERY

Nothing below is guessed and nothing is read off this stage's own data. Every input is a number in
a **committed artifact**; the probe **recomputes the whole derivation at run time** and **G-N**
fails if any frozen literal disagrees (the G-N form).

**Step 1 — the two committed rate sources** (#257.3(d): the census AND the T2-T0 smoke).

| zone | T2-C0 census rate (raw counts) | T2-C0 deliveries / team-match | T2-T0 smoke rate (raw counts) | T2-T0 smoke deliveries / team-match |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| own | 157 / 4,295 = **0.036554** | 10.7375 | 29 / 834 = **0.034772** | 834 / 80 = **10.425** |
| middle | 287 / 9,500 = **0.030211** | 23.75 | 47 / 1,989 = **0.023630** | 1,989 / 80 = **24.8625** |
| final | 40 / 2,066 = **0.019361** | 5.165 | 4 / 370 = **0.010811** | 370 / 80 = **4.625** |

**Step 2 — the CONSERVATIVE choice, per cell.** Delivery rates: the **lower** of the two per zone
⇒ **d = [10.425, 23.75, 4.625]** (fewer deliveries = less information = conservative, without
ambiguity). Punish rates: "lower per cell" is *not* unambiguously conservative for an ORDERING
(a lower middle rate widens the binding gap), so the rule is stated in its true form — **the power
is evaluated at BOTH committed rate vectors and the WORSE one binds**. The census vector binds at
every grid point: its `own − middle` gap is **0.634 pp** against the smoke's 1.114 pp, which is
exactly #257.3(d)'s binding pair.

**Step 3 — ⭐ THE CHAIN DESIGN EFFECT** (#256.3's rarity fact taken seriously). The label is
CHAIN-level: T2-C0's own accounting has **484 punished deliveries** sharing **286 attributed goals**
at the primary window, so the punished count is over-dispersed relative to binomial by
`deff = 484 / 286 = **1.692308**` (an equal-cluster LOWER bound, declared as such). The effective
per-zone book count is therefore

```
n_eff(z, M) = round( d_z · M / deff )
```

**Step 4 — q(M), the per-book ordering probability, EXACTLY.** With `K_z ~ Binomial(n_eff(z, M), p_z)`
independent,

```
q(M) = Σ_{k_m} P(K_m = k_m) · P(K_o/n_o > k_m/n_m) · P(K_f/n_f < k_m/n_m)
```

computed by exact binomial convolution over the middle zone (no normal approximation), with strict
inequalities (ties are NOT ordered).

**Step 5 — LIMB (ii)'s power.** `P(Bin(B, q) ≥ ceil(τ·B))`. Two readings are published: the
**CONSERVATIVE** one takes `B = R` (the two books of a replicate treated as ONE cluster — perfect
within-replicate correlation) and the **independent** one takes `B = 2R = 40`. Because the
threshold τ is a fixed *fraction*, the two readings are close, which is what makes the sizing
robust to the correlation assumption.

**Step 6 — LIMB (i)'s power.** Set grain = the replicate: `SE(gap) = sd_book(gap) / √R` with
`sd_book(gap)² = p_a(1−p_a)/n_eff(a) + p_b(1−p_b)/n_eff(b)`, two-sided 95 %.

**Step 7 — THE CONJUNCTION.** `power = power(i) × power(ii)` — a conservative product (the limbs
are positively correlated, so the true joint power is at least this).

**Step 8 — THE N RULE, frozen.**

```
R  = 20 replicates  ⇒ 2R = 40 books, ceil(0.9 · 40) = 36 ordered books required
M* = min(  smallest M ∈ {20, 40, …, 600} with min(power_census, power_smoke) ≥ 0.80,
           floor(SEED_ROOM / R) = floor(9000 / 20) = 450,          [the seed-ledger cap]
           floor(WALL_CAP_S · 1000 / (R · 3 arms · ms per walk))   [the declared wall cap] )
```

## §NON-CLAIMS

1. **NOTHING SHIPS.** Zero `src/**` bytes; the production fingerprint re-derived unchanged;
   `dvLearnedMap` stays a hard false in every production path.
2. ⭐ **THE EXAM SCORES THE SHAPE, NOT THE MAGNITUDE** (#246). A book that is ordered but sits
   below the census level passes; a book at census level but out of order fails.
3. **NO EVOLUTION, NO INHERITANCE, NO COACH, NO PER-PLAYER BOOKS, NO OPPONENT/IMITATION CLUSTERS**
   (contract §4). Rosters are fixed within a fixture and redrawn between fixtures; no genome is
   ever selected on.
4. **THE LEARN+CONSUME ARM SCORES NOTHING.** It is published — books, guards, feedback — and
   gates nothing. In particular it is **not** a second test of the registration.
5. **A WRONG BOOK IS LEGAL AND IS STYLE** (#247). The registration is about what the population of
   books grows, not about any one side being right.
6. **THE CONJUNCTION IS A PREDICATE, NOT A TRUTH.** τ = 0.90 is a frozen convention (#257.3(c)'s
   floor); a share of 0.875 is published as what it is.
7. **THE BINOMIAL SIZING IS A MODEL.** Delivery counts are random and labels are chain-level; the
   design effect corrects the numerator's over-dispersion by a lower-bound factor. Realised power
   can therefore be below the nominal 0.813 — declared here, before the run, rather than after it.
