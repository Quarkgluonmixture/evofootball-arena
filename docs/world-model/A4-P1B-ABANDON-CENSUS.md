units**, measured **interventionally** by a paired same-seed fork contrast. At a
sampled own-possession playing moment for side `d`, the match state is forked into
two INDEPENDENT deep copies:

* **branch A — the world AS-IS**: `d`'s index-1 body keeps the rest-defence
  designation (the incumbent policy: the PlayerBrain support-fan exclusion +
  the formations in-possession clamp both bind).
* **branch B — the designation DISABLED for side `d`**: from the fork tick,
  `abandonRestDesignation = d` REMOVES the policy — BOTH in-possession faces stop

The **policy price for the fork** = the excess downstream concession goal-value
that side `d` suffers in **branch B over branch A**, priced through the admitted
P1 surrogates over a pinned horizon (§2). A **positive** price means removing the
designation COSTS goals — the policy is worth something; a **null/negative** price

## §2 — THE FORK SAMPLING + THE HORIZON + THE ACCRUAL (DOSE) LADDER (frozen; no re-cut after sight)

### 2.1 The fork-moment rule (P2b idiom, pre-registered)

During a base (unforked) enriched eye-null match, a fork moment QUALIFIES at a
tick iff `m.phase === 'playing'` AND `m.ball.owner !== null` AND
`m.simTime − lastForkTime ≥ FORK_SPACING_S` (`FORK_SPACING_S = 4.0` s, the P2b
census cadence, pinned). The fork **side `d` = `owner.side`** (the side in
possession — the team whose rest defence is exposed on the coming turnover);
**both sides are eligible** (each is sampled whenever it owns). A pinned
**per-match cap `FORK_CAP_PER_MATCH = 40`** bounds wall (the smoke publishes the
realized forks/match and whether the cap binds); moments beyond the cap step
through unforked. `lastForkTime` resets on every QUALIFYING moment (P2b/V3-P0
placement). Receipts per fork (§7).

### 2.2 The paired fork (P2b idiom; ZERO leakage)

At a qualifying moment the base state `m` is forked into two INDEPENDENT deep
copies via `cloneSimulationState(m)` (the parent `m` is NEVER stepped inside the
fork — it continues its own loop unperturbed):

* **branch A** = `cloneSimulationState(m)`, stepped `W_MAX_TICKS` with no seam.
* **branch B** = `cloneSimulationState(m)`, `abandonRestDesignation := d` set on
  the clone, stepped `W_MAX_TICKS`.

Each branch is a self-contained Match; neither can touch the other or the parent.
**X-FORK-IDENT (HARD, 100% coverage):** an INDEPENDENT third plain clone stepped
`W_MAX_TICKS` must be byte-identical (harness signature) to branch A — proving the
as-is branch is a faithful continuation and the fork machinery perturbs nothing.

### 2.3 The horizon + the accrual (DOSE) ladder (pinned from published grids)

After the fork tick `t_fork`, count opponent concession surrogates suffered by `d`
in the half-open **`(t_fork, t_fork + W]`**, in each branch, on the null→true
entry transition SEEDED from the shared fork-start state (P2b F3 — only NEW entries
within `W` count; identical seeding across branches ⇒ a clean paired difference):

```text

                      breakaway is immediate — the same primary the A4-P1
                      vacancy census and the P2b hold read)
ACCRUAL LADDER  W ∈ {10 s, 20 s, 30 s}
                      10 s = the certified P0b concede horizon (primary);

                      midpoint. The DOSE / MONOTONE axis is this accrual ladder:
                      the paired price accrued over a GROWING horizon.
```

The intervention is binary (policy on / off), so the natural dose is the HORIZON
over which the effect accrues: a real rest-defence loss should GROW as the
