## §0 The diagnosis chain (why this arc, in football terms)

The tempo disease's deepest root (user-confirmed: 就是无脑抢): swarming is
RATIONAL in today's world because there is no dribble threat. In real football
pressing is disciplined by the FEAR OF BEING BEATEN — dive in, get touched
past, and the space behind you is torn open. Our engine has no meaningful way
for a carrier to beat an overcommitted defender, so diving in never loses;
with nothing to fear, all-swarm is the dominant strategy; hence the churn, the
2× pressed-loss ratio, the scramble the user sees. ⭐ THE GAME-THEORY TABLE IS
MISSING HALF ITS CARDS: defensive restraint cannot EMERGE (the vision's
requirement) until the world punishes recklessness — and that punishment is
the attacking capability this arc builds. Related: the futsal observation
(user) — small-format football's watchability leans on dribbling, which we
lack entirely; the format question (pitch × numbers) stays a SEPARATE
instrument question on the menu, not this arc's.

## §1 The claims (H-CB)

* **H-CB.1 (this arc's exam):** with commitment-honest dispossession and the
  directional touch-past built, BEATEN EVENTS become real, measurable world
  events — a defender arriving at speed can be eliminated for a physics-
  derived recovery interval by a well-timed, well-aimed touch that carries
  real cost (the ball leaves the feet; the race is real) — and they are
  VISIBLE in the live game view as real trajectories.
* **H-CB.2 (named for later arcs, not scored here):** given H-CB.1, defensive
  restraint becomes LEARNABLE (the defence account book: dove → beaten →
  punished) and dribble-vs-pass styles can DIVERGE by evolution — 纪律和风格
  长出来，不写死。

## §2 Mechanism (M-CB, dormant behind its own explicit flags; built through LAYER 2 only)

* **M-CB.1 — LAYER 1, THE PHYSICS OF COMMITMENT.** (a) The dispossession
  model becomes GEOMETRY-HONEST: a challenge's outcome depends on the
  defender's approach — speed, angle, turn-rate/momentum state (the engine's
  OWN motion constants: turn rates, accel/decel families — traced, never
  invented), not raw proximity. An overcommitted defender who misses is
  carried through by his own momentum and pays a physics-derived recovery
  interval (no invented stun timers — the time his own motion model needs to
  brake and turn). (b) THE DIRECTIONAL TOUCH-PAST: the carrier may knock the
  ball a real distance into chosen space; the ball genuinely leaves his feet
  (a loose-ball race anyone can win); success = geometry (his touch direction
  and speed vs the defender's recoverable cone), never a dice roll, never an
  attribute duel. All of it body knowledge (#248.1: bodies know physics —
  legitimately innate).
* **M-CB.2 — LAYER 2, THE CHOICE.** Touch-past candidates (direction ×
  timing) enter THE SAME candidate-pricing table as pass candidates — the
  banked pricer idiom, one table, no per-seam special cases; style weights
  (dribble-proneness) are GENES BORN ABSENT (style, the riskTolerance
  family); zero taste constants; the incumbent world byte-identical with the
  flags off.
* **M-CB.3 — ⭐ THE FRONTEND VISIBILITY RUNG (the user's clause).** The arc's
  deliverable includes making beaten events LEGIBLE in the live game view —
  rendered from what ACTUALLY HAPPENED (the real trajectories: the touch, the
  beaten defender's overrun and recovery arc), never canned animation. The
  presentation design (camera/highlight affordances, if any) is drafted at
  the frontend rung and lands at the play-test gate with everything else.
* **M-CB.4 — SCOPE.** Layer 3 (the defence book, carry-judgment learning,
  style evolution exams) = SEPARATE follow-on contracts reusing the proven
  learning canon. LAYER 4 IS PERMANENTLY OUT: no skill-move library, no
  animation mimicry, no attribute-vs-attribute dice duels (§-1 governs).

  will still churn faster than 11-a-side — the arc promises the DUEL, not a
  tempo target.
