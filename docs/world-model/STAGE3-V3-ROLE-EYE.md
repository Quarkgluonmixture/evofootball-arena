## 1. The measured case (all banked)

1. Three generations converged at fork grain for three measured reasons
   (v1 blind → v2 seeing motion → v2.1 braking), and the common root is
   SYMMETRY: the same table, the same weights, near-the-same percepts ⇒
   the same argmax. P2's 60.4% of deviations into one candidate is the
   signature of homogeneous minds, not of a bad table.
2. The incumbent's three victories are role-differentiation's: <br>
   `formationSpot` hands every role a DIFFERENT target. The eye kept
   challenging a role-divided baseline with a role-free map.
3. The composition price EXISTS and is geometric (V2-P1, certified,
   banked) — dropped from v3's table for power, not for truth.
4. Reality's ordering, per the user: role agreement comes FIRST (the
   pickup game's thirty seconds), refined doctrine later, per-moment
   micro-yielding (the brake) last. v3 enters at the first layer.

## 2. The amendment — THE ROLE AXIS

* The census table becomes `context × ROLE × candidate → approach value`,
  role ∈ {DF, MF, WG, ST} (GK never in the station family). Role is the
  world's OWN variable — read from the formation machinery's assignment,
  never authored, never re-mapped. The 12 v1 contexts stand; **the going
  axis is OUT** (#77.2(ii); the P1 anticipatory table stays banked
  certified and may re-enter by ruling).
* **The consumer reads HIS OWN role's column.** Own-state, no percept
  issue (a body knows his job — the A4 constraint honoured trivially).
  Symmetry breaks at the source: a DF and an ST at the same moment read
  different columns and argmax to different candidates BY CONSTRUCTION —
  exactly how reality prevents ten players sharing one map.
* Values stay MEASURED: "for a DF in this context, approaching X pays Y"
  is census output. The CONTENT of the division of labour emerges from
  prices; no tactic is written. (The emergence doctrine, intact at the
  exact spot three generations tested it.)

## 4. Stages (V3-P0…V3-P3; executor pre-registers each; commander
reviews; the standing pattern)

* **V3-P0 — THE ROLE BASE-RATE AND POWER MAP** (read-only, zero
  `src/**`): (i) station-family moment shares BY ROLE on the enriched
  world (the four-way population split the table's power hangs on);
  (ii) per-role context coverage (which context×role cells can ever
  reach 150 — the attainable-population arithmetic #24 demands,
  published before V3-P1 freezes); (iii) the INCUMBENT's role
  signature as the reference: where each role currently goes
  (deviation-mix baseline per role, the thing v3's consumer must
  differ from); (iv) the power arithmetic for the census budget
  (expect ≳ the V2-P1 scale; the sizing smoke rules).
* **V3-P1 — THE ROLE-CONDITIONED CENSUS**: the P1R instrument
  verbatim, cells keyed `context × role-of-the-forced-body ×
  candidate`. Pre-named hypothesis half 1 (#77.3): **role-conditioned
  prices DIFFER by role** — the census can SEE division of labour
  (pre-registered contrast: per (context, candidate), the spread of
  value across roles, with cluster CIs; the null = role does not
  separate prices, a real finding that kills v3 cheaply).
* **V3-P2 — THE CONSUMER**: each body reads his own role's column;
  five arms; DEV on the perceived-attainable denominator; pre-named
  hypothesis half 2 (#77.3): **deviation geometry SEPARATES by role
  and the convergence signature breaks by construction** (spacing not
  closing, duprun falling, per-role deviation mixes distinct) — the
  convergence mediators are PRIMARY this time; payoff
  secondary/reported (three generations of flat ATEs say the value
  question is downstream of the shape question).
* **V3-P3 — DEPLOYMENT + THE BATTERY + THE R3 ITERATION** (the v2 law
  verbatim).
