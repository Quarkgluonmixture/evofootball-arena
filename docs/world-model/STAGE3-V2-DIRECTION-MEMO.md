   table exactly as designed (42.5% deviation vs 44.4% predicted; context
   agreement 95.6%; PC resolves). The chooser, the seam, the fork harness
   and the mediator battery are reusable assets.
2. **The 40-cell payoff did not survive out of sample** (labelled data,
   half-width 0.005 — the winner's curse took nearly all of it).
3. ⭐ **The substrate law**: positional value censused one body at a time
   does not compose. Deployed together, bodies converge (spacing −2.33 m,
   duplicate runs 55.5→70.4%, rest defence 1.32→0.98, offsides +18.7%) —
   and there is a measured FEEDBACK LOOP: convergence raises perceived
   DENSITY, and crowded contexts carry the table's strongest prescriptions,
   so convergence feeds itself.
4. The one hopeful signal: the attack/defence shape delta TRIPLED — faces
   pricing differently is real — and C-BOX went POSITIVE (+19.5% fuller at
   cross arrival). The eye moves bodies somewhere football wants them; it
   just cannot see what its neighbours are doing.

## 2. The proposed direction: THE ANTICIPATORY CENSUS

**One sentence: the context gains a compositional feature — how many
teammates are ALREADY GOING to the same region, read from the body's own
percept — so the table prices the MARGINAL approach, and the second body
converging on a lane sees a cell that tells him not to.**

* **The census stays unilateral and fork-and-force** (the population law
  survives): force ONE body's approach as before, but RECORD, at each
  moment, the anticipatory density of the candidate region — perceived own
  bodies whose motion points into it within the window. The table becomes
  `(context × others-going) → value`. No deployment is needed to build it:
  "others going there" already happens at natural rates in the incumbent
  world; it becomes a CONDITION, not a treatment.
* **Why this breaks the feedback loop instead of feeding it**: v1's DENSITY
  counted static bodies within 9 m — convergence made cells look crowded
  AFTER the pile-up formed, and crowded cells prescribed the strongest
  pushes. v2's feature reads MOTION — the pile-up is visible while it is
  still forming, and the marginal value of being the second arrival is a
  measured number (expected: sharply negative — that is the composition
  price v1 could not see).
* **Percept-honest by construction**: teammate motion comes from the body's
  own snapshot (two remembered positions differenced if the percept carries
  no velocity — anticipation as perception work, which is exactly what
  #44.4 named). No truth, no oracle, same E-series pull semantics.
* **One adoption-level iteration, not a fixed-point chase** (the §4.5.2
  law): after the v2 chooser passes its fork test, the census RE-RUNS once
  at R3 saturation (all bodies consuming the table) and the table's
  stability under its own deployment is a GATE. If the world the table
  creates invalidates the table, that is a measured FAIL, not a surprise.
* **Two harness repairs carried from #44.2(v), fixed at contract time**:
  the in-flight no-owner windows (28.7% of decisions — FACE needs either
  last-perceived-owner retention or an explicit in-flight face) and the
  first-window no-snapshot defect (20.5% — forks warm the percept before
  the first decision).
