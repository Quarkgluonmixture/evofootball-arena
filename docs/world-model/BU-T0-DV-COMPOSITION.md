## §LIFECYCLE — ⭐⭐ THE #269.2(iv) DEBT, AND IT RUNS FIRST

**The order of proof is binding (#286.3): this precedes the battery.** A staleness defect at the
authorized composition **REFUSES THE RUN (exit 4)** and reports for adjudication — a fix is a
`src` change needing its own authorization.

### The class, stated exactly

`Match.forcedTouchPast` is a **SINGLE match-scoped slot**. It is WRITTEN by `armTouchPast` from
the ONE call site in `PlayerBrain` (the CB-T2 choice seat), WITHDRAWN by `clearTouchPastArming` at
that same site when the body's next decision no longer wants the knock, and CONSUMED by the ONE
fork in `Match.stepBall`. **The debt**: a world that arms OTHER seams beside it may take an
**EARLY RETURN above the seat's block**, so the withdrawal never runs and an aim survives its own
tick — and a surviving aim can fire into a **LATER possession**, i.e. stale.

### How it is proven, without touching `src`

The decision loop and `stepBall` both run **inside** `Match.step`, so in a clean lifecycle the
slot is **always null when `step` returns**. Every non-null observation at a step boundary is a
**CARRY-OVER**, and each is measured for the three ways it could leak: across a change of ball
**OWNER**, across a change of **PHASE**, and live at the **WHISTLE** — plus the two end facts
(null at construction, so nothing enters a match; null at full time, so nothing leaves one).
