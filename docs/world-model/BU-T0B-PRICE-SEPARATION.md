### The counterfactual, and how it is kept from ever looking like truth

The counterfactual enters **through the dose and nowhere else**, by the shipped writer (the
BU-T0 idiom, house law #270). `DeliveryAccountBook` serves `punished[z] / deliveries[z]`, so a
λ-dosed book keeps the TRUE delivery count `D_z` and writes `round(λ · P_z)` punished labels
through the book's own public `note()`. A λ-dosed book is a state the world **could** have
reached: it is the book of a team that was punished λ times as often into the same zones. No
field surgery, no new capability, nothing anywhere near `info.genome`.

⚠ It is labelled counterfactual in **four** places, so it can never be confused with
truth-dosing: the probe header, the artifact's top-level `counterfactualWarning`, the
`counterfactual: true` flag on every rung row, and this document. **The yardstick face
definitions are UNCHANGED from BU-T0** (#256.2) — commensurability is what makes the response
curve readable at all.

### ⭐⭐ THE LINEARITY ANALYSIS — read from the source BEFORE the ladder was fixed

The price the chooser subtracts is, at the candidate's own aim
(`deliveryRiskPrice`, `src/ai/deliveryValueSeat.ts`):

```text
price = exposureWeight · exposure(from, aim)  +  belief[zone(aim)] · valueScale
                         └─ NOT ARMED (M-DV2.4) ⇒ exactly +0 ─┘
```

so the live term is exactly `belief[zone] · passBase`, **linear in the belief**, subtracted as
the last statement of `PlayerBrain`'s hoisted `groundCandidate`. **There is no clamp on the
price itself and no clamp on the candidate score `s` downstream of the subtraction** — the
argmax consumes `s − price` raw. But **two clamps stand upstream of the belief**, and they bind
at exactly the same place:

1. `dvLossBeliefVector` (`src/evolution/genome.ts`) runs every slot through **`clamp01`**;
2. the book itself cannot serve more than 1 — **`punished[z] ≤ deliveries[z]`** by the writer's
   own construction.

⭐ **THE LINEAR REGION IS THEREFORE EXACTLY**

```text
λ  ≤  λ_LIN  =  min over evidenced zones of  D_z / P_z  =  1 / max_z belief_true_z
             =  182,954 / 7,664  =  23.87186847599165        (the OWN third binds)
```

⭐ **λ_LIN IS ALSO THE SATURATION POINT, and that is arithmetic, not luck.** Above it the own
third is pinned at 1 while the middle and final thirds keep climbing toward the same ceiling,
so the *cross-zone differential* — the thing that can re-route a pass — **decreases**. Its
derivative just above λ_LIN is `−2·b_final/3 < 0`. **No dose through this seam is ever louder
than λ_LIN.** Any rung that would leave the region is capped **at** the boundary and the
capping is published as a fact of record (`gLadder` asserts it).

### ⭐⭐ THE TWO LOUDNESS AXES — the arithmetic that makes this stage readable

A price charged **equally** to every candidate cancels in an argmax. So the map has two
distinct ways to move a chooser, with **different ceilings**:

| axis | what it moves | the quantity |
|---|---|---|
| **DIFFERENTIAL** | *which zone* to pass into | mean over the three unordered zone pairs of `\|price_i − price_j\|` |
| **LEVEL** | *whether to pass at all* | the price itself — the DV term is subtracted from **ground-pass candidates only**, never from Dribble / Shoot / Clear, so its full level moves pass-vs-not-pass |

Both are published at every rung. λ3 is matched on the **DIFFERENTIAL** axis, per the
dispatch's own words.
