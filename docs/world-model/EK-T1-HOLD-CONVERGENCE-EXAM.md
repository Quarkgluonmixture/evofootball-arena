   deterministic; `deff = 1.613402` is an equal-cluster LOWER bound.
4. **G-ARMS' `doorsShut` LIMB IS SCOPED**: the MT world-flag set OVERLAPS this world's own census
   flags (the two percept doors belong to both), so the MT limb covers the flags the drill world
   does **not** arm; the census flags are gated by their own `drillWorld` conjunct.
5. ⭐ **G-VALUES-UNREACHABLE'S FORM FLOOR IS SHARPENED AND PUBLISHED**: EK-T0's floor counted
   decimals alone, which sufficed while its 2-dp percentage form was always dropped by that same

   ×100 form of a very small value ("0.00012" → "0.012") carries two significant digits and
   collides with ordinary engine constants by arithmetic accident, so the floor now also requires
   **four significant digits**. The excluded count is published beside the gate.
6. **THE SMOKE MODE OWNS ITS OWN CANONICAL ARTIFACT AND ITS OWN SEED SUB-BLOCK** (the EK-C0 family
   form): `EKT1_MODE` is not an override, every other variable is.
7. **THE GUARD SAMPLER RUNS IN EVERY ARM** and its inertness is proved on the twin block, so the
   byte-identity claim cannot hide a perturbing instrument.
8. ⭐⭐ **TWO REAL INSTRUMENT FAULTS WERE CAUGHT BY THIS STAGE'S OWN GATES AT SMOKE SCALE, BEFORE
   THE FROZEN RUN** (the EK-C0b idiom — a stage's smoke is there to fail):
