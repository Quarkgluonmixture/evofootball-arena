### ⭐ THE REPLICATE, THE SEASON AND THE CLUSTER

A **replicate** = ONE persistent `l3DefenceBooks` pair driven across its whole match sequence;
`League.startSeason()` never fires (the probe drives the sequence directly), so M-L3.2's season-reset
clause is **honoured by the probe, not amended** — the EK-T1/L3-T1 precedent, verbatim.
**A SEASON = 7 FIXTURES PER TEAM** (traced from `League.ts`: eight clubs, single round-robin).

⭐⭐ **THE CLUSTER IS THE (replicate, season) BLOCK — 7 fixtures = 14 team-matches.** It is chosen
because it is **the unit of BOOK STATE**: in arm A and arm B the books are wiped at the boundary and
in arm C they are re-dosed there, so a season block carries **no** state into the next one. (The
replicate is *not* the right cluster: with every arm's memory reset each season, replicate-grain
clustering would throw away 15× the resolution for nothing. Declared here, before the run.)
**K = 8 × 15 = 120 clusters per arm**, and the arms are **PAIRED**: every arm walks the SAME 840
seeds (`gClusters`).

## §M — ⭐ THE EX-ANTE SIZING, EVERY STEP, FROZEN BEFORE THE BATTERY

The primary quantity is the **group-level lunge-rate contrast** (arm B/C vs arm A, paired by
cluster). Its ex-ante MDE is derived from **committed moments only** — nothing is read off this
stage's own battery, and the probe **recomputes the whole derivation at run time** (`gSizing` fails
if any frozen literal disagrees).

**Step 1 — THE COMMITTED MOMENTS.** L3-C0's committed **g2 `lungesPerTeamMatch`** row (its
corrections leave it unamended; 316 team-matches of the polished armed world):

| group | mean / team-match | sd |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| CONTROLLED (< v\*) | **14.712025** | **6.654872** |
| RECKLESS (≥ v\*) | **2.085443** | **1.575819** |

**Step 2 — THE OVER-DISPERSION FACTOR.** Team-matches inside one season block are not independent
(one restart resolves several of a team's events at once — #280.2(i)'s own mechanism). The variance
is inflated by **L3-T1's committed `deff` = 1.875**, read from its artifact. ⚠ Declared as a MODEL
(§NON-CLAIMS 5): `deff` was measured for *label clustering*, and re-using it for *lunge counts* is a
conservative import, not a measurement of this quantity.

**Step 3 — THE ARITHMETIC, in full.**

```text
sd(cluster mean)  = sd(team-match) / sqrt(14) × sqrt(deff)
se(paired)        = sqrt(2) × sd(cluster mean) / sqrt(K)      [K = 120; sqrt(2) is CONSERVATIVE:
                                                               pairing on identical seeds can only
                                                               reduce the difference's variance]
MDE (80 % power, two-sided 5 %) = (z_.975 + z_.80) × se(paired) = 2.8015852 × se(paired)
```

**THE RESULT, FROZEN HERE:**

| quantity | CONTROLLED | RECKLESS |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| sd(cluster mean) | 2.4354338 | 0.5766907 |
| se(paired contrast) | 0.3144131 | 0.0744504 |
| ⭐ **MDE, per team-match** | **0.8808552** | **0.2085793** |
| ⭐ **MDE, relative to the OFF level** | **5.99 %** | **10.00 %** |

⭐ **THE HONEST HEADLINE OF THE SIZING**: at 840 seeds × 4 arms this battery can resolve a **~6 %**
change in the controlled lunge rate and a **~10 %** change in the reckless one. It is sized for the
MECHANISM READ. **The world faces (spells, pressed share, fouls, goals, duels) get CIs from the same
