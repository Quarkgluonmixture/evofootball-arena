## §6 — SIZING-BEFORE-FLOORS (the smoke → the frozen N arithmetic → the cap)

The DEGEN-RESTDEF limb is the binding gate (leg i); the frontier PASS turns on its
`PRIOR − R3p` CI excluding zero. The other legs are resolved-sign shape checks
(over-powered at the P3p-3 scale). So the smoke sizes the DEGEN-RESTDEF contrast.

* **The smoke** — 40 sets @ 12.208M (§7), all five arms, the light instruments,
  X-DET double-run. Publishes: the per-match SD of the paired `PRIOR − R3p`
  restSlot diff (`σ̂`), its pooled point + cluster CI, the per-set wall, and the
  frozen N arithmetic. (The light restSlot is the per-tick surrogate; the battery
  uses the full 6 Hz possession-sampled I5(b) — over-powered vs the surrogate, so
  the smoke SD is a conservative sizing anchor.)
* **The frozen N rule** (P3p-3 form). `MDL = min( 0.5·|smoke PRIOR−R3p restSlot
  point| , 0.01 )` (designated-slot-occupancy units; the 0.01 floor = one
  percentage-point of I5(b) occupancy — a modest but material closing of the
  −0.161 deficit; the `0.5·|point|` guard stops noise inflating it). `N* = the
  smallest 200-step match count with `SE_N = σ̂/√N ≤ MDL / POWER_Z`` (POWER_Z =
  3.605 = z.975 + z.95 — a two-sided 95 % CI test at 95 % power to resolve the
  improvement away from zero), **CAPPED at N_MAX**.
* **N_MAX is WALL-DERIVED** at the smoke: the largest 200-step N whose projected
  total battery wall (`per-set wall × N × 2` for X-DET) ≤ **12 hours**, itself
  capped at the hard **N_CAP = 8,000** (which keeps the battery band ≤ 12,217,999,
  inside the reservation — §7).
* **The attainability knee + the reduced-power disclosure rule.** If the power
  rule projects `N* > N_MAX`, the cap binds and the reduced-power reading is
  DISCLOSED BEFORE the gate-bearing run (no optional stopping, #105.4); the
  DEGEN-RESTDEF CI is REPORTED at whatever power the cap delivers, and the frontier
  gate reads leg (i) from that CI (resolved-away-from-zero, or UNRESOLVED). If a
  gate stratum is genuinely unmeasured, a #44.5 sizing sign-off returns to the
  commander before the run (it should not — every instrument reuses P3p-3).

**Band anchoring (#68.2).** The P3p-3 §RESULT numbers are sizing anchors only; the
battery re-measures every quantity on its own fresh R0 / R3p at analysis.
