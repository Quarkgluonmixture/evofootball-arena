## §1 — THE ESTIMAND (what A4-P1c measures, and why the intervention runs the GRANT direction)

**The CAUSAL discipline value of a GRANTED back home-region prior on the EYE
world**, measured **interventionally** by a paired same-seed fork contrast, on
**RAW opponent deep-entry rates against the granted side**. At a sampled
own-possession playing moment for side `d`, the R3p base state is forked into
INDEPENDENT deep copies:

* **branch A — R3p AS-IS**: `homeRegionGrant` null (the per-tick eye value gradient
  governs the granted body's stations, with NO positional prior).
* **branch B_dose — R3p + the GRANT for side `d`**: `homeRegionGrant =
  {side:d, bodyIndex:1, strength:dose}` on the clone — a distance-decayed SOFT bias
  toward the back home region is added to side `d`'s index-1 body's per-candidate

  consumption moment; the DF keeps his ordinary machinery — STATUE-safe). One clone
  per DOSE (multi-branch per fork, shared branch A ⇒ the whole dose ladder is
  same-seed paired).

The **grant's effect for a fork** = the change in downstream opponent deep entries
against `d` in **branch B over branch A**, in RAW rate. A **negative** delta means
the grant RECOVERS discipline (the granted body stays home, fewer deep entries); a
**null/positive** delta means the grant buys no discipline.

**Why the GRANT direction (not the ABANDON direction of P1/P1b).** P1 and P1b both

rescoped contract §4 names.
