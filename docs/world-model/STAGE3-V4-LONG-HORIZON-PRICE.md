## 2. The amendment — THE MECHANISM CLASSES

Not every fired limb is the same disease. v4 freezes THREE mechanism
classes ex ante; V4-P0 routes each limb to its class BY MEASUREMENT
(routing criteria pre-registered before any mining). The suspicions
below are named priors, never verdicts.

* **CLASS H — HEDGE / HORIZON** (suspected: rest-defence slot; the
  delivery economy). The job's value binds rarely and late — invisible
  at 6/10 s by construction. Remedy: **the occupancy census**
  (fork-and-hold: force ONE body to keep its designated station) with
  outcomes read through CALIBRATED SURROGATES at long windows, so the
  rare late value becomes a measurable price in goal-value units.
* **CLASS S — STATE-BLIND** (suspected: offsides). The cost IS inside
  the existing horizons but is averaged away because the context key
  cannot distinguish the states (a run beyond the line and one just
  onside share a cell; the census, sampled on incumbent play, rarely
  visits the deep-beyond-line corner the eye now reaches). Remedy: a
  context-axis extension — the SAME instrument, one new percept-honest
  bit (e.g. an offside-relative flag read from PERCEIVED opponents,
  with a named abstention class).
* **CLASS J — JURISDICTION / SUPPORT** (suspected: restarts). The
  consumer reads the table at moments outside the census's sampled
  support — extrapolation, not pricing (the census samples open-play
  station-family moments; a restart phase is neither). Remedy: **the
  in-support consumption law** — v3's I7 finished: the consumer reads
  in-power, IN-SUPPORT cells only, generically (never a named-phase
  carve-out; restart phases fall out of the law only if P0 shows they
  are out of support — if they are IN support and mispriced, the limb
  routes to S or H instead).

The CONTENT of discipline still emerges: "for a DF in this context,
holding the designated post pays Y over 30 s" is census output. No
tactic is written; discipline must become CHEAPEST AT TRUE PRICES or
the battery will say it did not.

## 4. Stages (V4-P0…V4-P4; executor pre-registers each; commander
reviews; the standing pattern)

* **V4-P0 — THE AUTOPSY MAP** (read-only, zero `src/**`): (i) map the
  world's OWN designation variables (the rest-defence assignment, the
  restart stations) to file:line; (ii) mine the P3a corpus (labelled,
  I7): localise each fired limb in event time — the time-to-cost
  curves (when does the cost bind relative to the abandonment), the
  carrying channels (opponent deep entries / shots against / delivery
  events), the contexts and roles; (iii) ROUTE each limb to H/S/J by
  the pre-registered criteria (frozen before mining; an unroutable
  limb stops the stage at the commander); (iv) binding-moment base
  rates on a FRESH incumbent reference (possession-loss transitions,
  pass-release-near-line moments, restart phases, delivery build-ups
  per match) — the power arithmetic fuel; (v) the CLASS S test for
  offsides: within existing census cells, do beyond-line and onside
  sub-moments carry resolvedly different realised outcomes (visible
  but averaged away), or is the cost genuinely absent at 6/10 s?
* **V4-P1 — THE CALIBRATION** (CLASS H prerequisite; observational, no
  forcing): measure the surrogate → goal-value links on the enriched
  world (concession hazard following opponent deep/box entry and shots
  against; goal hazard following delivery events), cluster CIs, table
  SHA'd. Gate: a surrogate enters only with a resolved monotone link;
  failures are published and dropped (I3).
* **V4-P2 — THE OCCUPANCY CENSUS** (CLASS H, the big instrument):
  fork-and-hold at sampled binding moments — force ONE body to hold
  its own designation for W_hold; paired same-seed control; outcome =
  calibrated surrogates over W_long (both windows pinned ex ante from
  P0's curves); cells keyed context × role × designation-class; sizing
  smoke first (I5). Pre-named hypothesis: **occupancy of the
  designated long-horizon stations carries a resolved nonzero price in
  goal-value units.** The null is a real finding that stops CLASS H at
  the commander BEFORE any consumer is built.
* **V4-P3 — THE MERGED CONSUMER**: one scalar = the v3 role-table
  two-face read + the calibrated long-horizon terms (CLASS H) + the
  context extensions (CLASS S) + the in-support law (CLASS J); the
  five-arm form. PRIMARY mediators at fork grain: the three fired
  limbs (designated-slot occupancy, offside rate, restart resettle) —
  AND the shape mediators re-run (the cure must not regress). Payoff
  reported, secondary.
* **V4-P4 — DEPLOYMENT + THE BATTERY + THE R3 ITERATION** (the v2 law
  verbatim): the P3a battery re-run with the three limbs as named
  gates at their exact bands; §2 band; the adoption ladder; the R3
  iteration once. Nothing ships without the user's eyes.

## 6. Stop rules

Any X-family/fidelity gate fails ⇒ FAIL, stop at the commander. An
unroutable limb at V4-P0 ⇒ stop at the commander. A CLASS H term whose
calibration fails does not enter (I3); if ALL CLASS H terms fail
calibration, the stage stops at the commander (the registered fallback
— pricing discipline through selection, or through an A4 doctrine
slice — is a FUTURE contract, not this one). V4-P2's null stops
CLASS H before the consumer. Any battery limb at V4-P4 fires ⇒ stop
outright. No re-cutting after sight. The R3 iteration runs once.
Nothing ships (Road B): every flag dormant, fingerprint unchanged,
throughout.

## 7. Registered non-claims

v4 makes no deployment claim before V4-P4 and no shipping claim before
the user's eyes. v4 does NOT claim discipline "should" win: if honest
prices say the designated post is not worth holding, the battery still
rules and the finding is banked as written. No doctrine layer is
authored (pressing triggers, cover assignments, set-piece jobs remain
future A4 slices, each its own contract). The delivery economy may
resolve as DOWNSTREAM of the other limbs (a registered possibility:
P0 may route it to "downstream-watch" with no term of its own — the
battery decides). If disciplined, role-shaped, rest-defending football
appears, it EMERGED from measured prices consumed through honest eyes
— which is the point.
