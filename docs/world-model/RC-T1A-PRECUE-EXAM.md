the passer's body squared up to him treats the strike as something he half expected: he waits
**17 ticks instead of 27** at the best rank. This exam opens that door and asks one question —

lands. If a shorter wait does not close some of that, the shape of the mechanism is wrong and

passes** and his team must not **give the ball away more**.

programme has used — nobody has learnt anything yet, so every reaction pays the long wait, and
the door has the most room it will ever have. That form is where the hypothesis is SCORED. The
