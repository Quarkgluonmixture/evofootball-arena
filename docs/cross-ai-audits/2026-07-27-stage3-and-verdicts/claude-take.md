## (a) STAGE3-POSITIONING-EYE.md

1. **Effect size is the unconfronted risk (my top concern).** Every E5-family
   census measured DIRECT causes (pass target choice → shot within 4 s),
   effects 1–15pp. A station offset is a WEAK, slow cause: one off-ball body
   10 m from the ball for a ~2 s window. Plausible per-candidate effects on
   shot-within-H are sub-pp. The contract inherits C3R floor discipline and
   #24 attainability but never asks "is the SIGNAL bigger than the world's
   noise at any affordable budget?" — the #29.5 family (a gate that cannot
   fire / a census that cannot resolve). P1's pre-registration MUST carry an
   ex-ante MDE-vs-expected-effect calculation, with a designed answer for
   "signal too small" (longer windows W, station persistence, paired
   same-seed forks to cancel world noise — the paired design is the likely
   rescue and the contract doesn't name it).
2. **The gene→weight mapping is a hand choice wearing gene clothing.** Q6
   freezes a mapping from existing tactical genes to face weights. If the
   MAPPING is wrong, P2/P3 read "the eye loses to the incumbent" when the
   truth is "the mapping loses". Attribution needs an ablation family in P2:
   neutral-weight eye vs gene-mapped eye vs incumbent. Without it a FAIL is
   uninterpretable and a whole slice could be wrongly closed.
3. **Face-asymmetric horizons.** Score-face value accrues fast (shots);
   concede-face value accrues slower (defensive shape prevents chances that
   materialize later). One shared H biases the table toward attack; gene
   weights would then inherit a structural attack tilt. Either face-specific
   H (pre-registered) or an explicit test that the concede face has any
   gradient at the chosen H.
4. **Anticipatory density is missing.** Density-as-feature prices TODAY's
   crowding, but simultaneous consumers move on the same table: everyone sees
   the same underpriced cell, all move, density materializes only after
   arrival. Commitment windows dampen oscillation but not first-wave
   pile-ups. The honest cheap fix is perceived-teammate-MOTION as a feature
   (a body moving toward a zone raises its expected density) — perception-
   legal, census-measurable. If P2's instruments show pile-ups, this is the
   named lever; the contract should say so rather than leave it to a redraw.
5. **P0's cadence circularity (minor).** Commitment windows derive from the
   INCUMBENT's measured cadence; the eye's stability needs may differ. Fine
   as a starting point; flag it as a derived-not-eternal constant.

## (b) C5 T1 — "nothing pays a held tick back"

1. **The twin closes the window-asymmetry attack, and I re-verified the
   logic**: decision-moment origin mechanically charges hold-k arms k/240 of
   the window; the release-origin twin removes that asymmetry and still
   reads −6.5/−10.2/−12.4pp. The mechanical component is real but bounded;
   the residual is world-truth. The verdict survives my strongest attack.
2. **Residual caveat — the forced hold is unconditional.** Real holding is
   SELECTIVE. All three pressure strata are negative, so no measured stratum
   rescues it, but the strata are coarse (pressure × stale × support). The
   unpark condition (any cell's interval reaching zero) already handles
   this; the parking is sound, the phrase "never pays" should always carry
   "at measured granularity".
3. **Check-worthy detail**: whether the forced hold arms invoked the T0
   shield-hold (body position + protective drift) or a bare stand-still. A
   bare stand-still would be a strawman of waiting. My reading of the chain
   says T0's built Hold action is what T1 forces, but this deserves a
   citation-level verification.
